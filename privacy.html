Privacy & Digital Data Protection Policy

1. Data Minimization & PCI-DSS Compliance

BharatRail strictly complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and PCI-DSS standards. We never store raw payment credentials, CVV codes, or UPI PINs. Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using bcrypt.

2. Passenger Information Handling & Charting

Passenger names, age, gender, and berth preferences are collected solely for generating authoritative railway reservation charts and Electronic Reservation Slips (ERS). Data is encrypted in transit using 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol.

3. Immutable Audit Logs & Fraud Prevention

All reservation requests, cancellations, and status modifications are recorded in tamper-evident audit logs to safeguard against unauthorized mass bot bookings and ensure genuine public ticket accessibility.
