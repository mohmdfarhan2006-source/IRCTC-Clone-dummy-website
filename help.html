Frequently Asked Reservation Questions

Q: What is the difference between GNWL, RLWL, and PQWL?

GNWL (General Waitlist): Has the highest confirmation probability. Allocated for passengers beginning their journey at the train’s origin terminal.
RLWL (Remote Location Waitlist): Dedicated quota pool between intermediate key junction stations.
PQWL (Pooled Quota Waitlist): Shared across several intermediate small halts that pool a minor reservation quota.

Q: When does Tatkal reservation open?

Tatkal reservation opens 1 day prior to the train departure from originating station: 10:00 AM IST for AC Classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC) and 11:00 AM IST for Non-AC Classes (SL, 2S).

Q: What are the berth layouts in 2A vs Chair Cars (CC / EC)?

AC 2 Tier (2A): Strictly 4 berths per bay (Lower, Upper, Side Lower, Side Upper). There is no Middle Berth in 2A coaches.
Vande Bharat / Shatabdi Chair Cars (CC & EC): Equipped with ergonomic seating only (Window and Aisle seats). There are no sleeping berths in day trainsets.
