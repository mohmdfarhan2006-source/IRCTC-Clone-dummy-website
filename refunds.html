Cancellation Time Windows & Deduction Slabs

Between 48 hours and 12 hours before scheduled departure:

Cancellation charge is 25% of the total fare (subject to the minimum flat class charge above).

Between 12 hours and up to 4 hours before scheduled departure:

Cancellation charge is 50% of the total fare (subject to the minimum flat class charge).

Less than 4 hours before departure or after chart preparation:

No refund is admissible on confirmed tickets once reservation charts are prepared.
