Cancellation Time Windows & Sliding Slabs

Between 48 hours and 12 hours before scheduled departure: Cancellation charge is 25% of the total fare (subject to the minimum flat class charge).
Between 12 hours and up to 4 hours before scheduled departure: Cancellation charge is 50% of the total fare (subject to the minimum flat class charge).
Less than 4 hours before departure or post chart preparation: No statutory refund is admissible on confirmed tickets once reservation charts are prepared. Online TDR must be filed for train cancellations or railway service deficiencies.
