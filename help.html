Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between GNWL, RLWL, and PQWL?

GNWL (General Waitlist): Highest confirmation probability. Assigned for journeys originating from the train’s primary source station.
RLWL (Remote Location Waitlist): Assigned for journeys between intermediate major stations.
PQWL (Pooled Quota Waitlist): Shared across several intermediate stations that pool a smaller reservation quota.

Q: When does Tatkal reservation open?

Tatkal booking opens 1 day prior to the train’s departure date: 10:00 AM IST for AC classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC) and 11:00 AM IST for Non-AC classes (SL, 2S).

Q: What is the Single Availability Guarantee on BharatRail?

Every reservation enquiry strictly returns one unambiguous, authoritative state (e.g. AVAILABLE 31, RAC 6, or GNWL 14). Contradictory states like RAC + GNWL or CONFIRMED + WAITLIST are architecturally prohibited.
