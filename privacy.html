Privacy & Data Protection Policy

1. Data Minimization & Security

BharatRail complies with standard data protection protocols. We never store raw payment credentials, CVV codes, or UPI PINs. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using salted bcrypt keys.

2. Passenger Information Handling

Passenger names, ages, and berth preferences are collected solely for generating official railway charts and reservations. Data is stored in secure, encrypted relational storage.

3. Audit Logs & Telemetry

All booking creations, cancellations, and status modifications are recorded in immutable audit logs to protect against fraudulent reservation requests.
