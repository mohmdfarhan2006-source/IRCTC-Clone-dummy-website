Terms of Service & Reservation Guidelines

1. Acceptance of Terms

By booking train tickets on the BharatRail passenger portal, you agree to adhere to the Indian Railways Act and commercial reservation guidelines governing passenger carriage, fare tariffs, and ticket cancellations.

2. Authoritative Timetables & Stoppages

All train timetables, route sequencing, running days, and intermediate stoppages are ingested from authoritative railway datasets. Passenger boarding and deboarding are restricted to confirmed stoppages recorded on the Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS).

3. Identity Verification

At least one passenger on an ERS must produce a valid government-approved original photo identification (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID, PAN card, Student ID) upon demand by the Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE).

4. Quotas & Waitlist Regulations

General (GN), Tatkal (TQ), Premium Tatkal (PT), Ladies (LD), and Senior Citizen (SS) allocations operate under separate statutory quotas. Waitlisted passengers without confirmed or RAC status after chart preparation are not permitted to board reserved coaches.
